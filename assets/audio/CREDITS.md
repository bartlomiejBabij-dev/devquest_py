## Original effects

`correct.wav` (rising spell), `incorrect.wav` (soft descending spell), `victory.wav` (short fanfare), and `boss-victory.wav` (extended chord fanfare) were synthesized for DevQuest using the original project script `scripts/create_audio.py`. No third-party recordings, samples, or copied melodies are used. No third-party attribution obligations apply. These project-created assets can be used with DevQuest commercially.

Format: 22,050 Hz, mono, signed 16-bit PCM WAV. Reproduce with `python scripts/create_audio.py`. Rounded envelopes and peak limiting are applied in the script. Music/effect playback volumes are independently adjustable; the music files themselves are unmodified.
